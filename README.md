# Privacy Policy

**Application:** Secure QR Message

**Developer:** Inlihalabs

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Inlihalabs respects your privacy. Secure QR Message is designed to help you create, share and unlock encrypted messages while keeping your personal information under your control.

This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information and uses device permissions.

## 2. Personal Information

Secure QR Message does not require users to create an account, register or sign in.

The application does not collect, store on external servers or sell your personal information.

The application does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking tools or third-party crash reporting services.

## 3. Message Encryption and Local Data Storage

All message encryption and decryption processes are performed locally on your device.

Your messages, passwords, hints and creator nicknames are not transmitted to Inlihalabs or any external server by the application.

The application stores certain information locally on your device, including:

- Created message history, including message content, passwords, hints and creator nicknames.
- Free message quota and usage information.
- Application settings and preferences.

Message history can be viewed, managed and deleted within the application.

Please note that encryption protects the secret content of the QR message. Optional hints and creator nicknames are not encrypted.

## 4. Internet Connection

An internet connection is used to obtain accurate time information for managing the monthly free message quota.

External time services may receive your IP address as part of normal network communication.

The application does not intentionally transmit personal information, device identifiers, message content or passwords to these services.

Creating and unlocking Secure QR Messages can be performed without an internet connection, subject to the available message quota.

## 5. Device Permissions

Secure QR Message may request access to your photo library or device storage for the following purposes:

- Saving generated QR images to your device.
- Selecting existing QR images for message decryption.

The application does not upload your photos or QR images to external servers.

Camera access is not required.

## 6. Sharing QR Images

You may choose to share generated QR images through messaging applications, social media platforms, email services or other applications.

When you share an image through another application, the handling of that image is subject to the privacy policy of the selected application or service.

Inlihalabs does not control how third-party applications handle shared images.

## 7. Data Security and User Responsibility

Secure QR Message uses encryption to protect the secret content of your messages.

The security of an encrypted message also depends on the strength of the password you choose.

Users are encouraged to choose strong passwords and keep them secure.

There is no master password or password recovery mechanism for retrieving forgotten passwords from encrypted QR images.

Users are responsible for protecting their devices, locally stored message history and shared passwords.

No security technology can guarantee absolute protection against all possible threats.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by the application is maintained locally on your device.

You can delete individual messages or clear your entire message history within the application.

Deleting message history does not automatically delete QR images previously saved or shared.

Saved QR images can be deleted using your device's photo library or file management tools.

Inlihalabs does not maintain a remote database of your messages or personal information.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
